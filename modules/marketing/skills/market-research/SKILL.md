# Market research

Ask for the company or product, known competitors, and `quick` or `deep` depth. Create a run directory and
write findings under `intelligence/competitors/<name>/` and `intelligence/personas/<name>/`.

Use scouts with `model: sonnet`. Give each scout one brief from `references/` and the shared inputs. Quick
research uses discovery, positioning, and synthesis. Deep research also uses sales intelligence, customer
language, SEO, and persona briefs. Require links and dates for external claims.

The synthesizer combines evidence into short tables. It must separate facts, inferences, and unknowns.
