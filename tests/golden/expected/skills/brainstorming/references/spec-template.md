# Spec Template

Use this structure for the Phase 5 spec document. Never leave a section as "TBD" — unresolved
items go in Open Questions with context on what must be decided and who can decide it.

---

# Spec: &lt;Feature Name&gt;

## Problem Statement
What exists today and why it needs to change.

## Chosen Approach
The selected approach with rationale for why it was chosen over the alternatives.

## Key Decisions
Decisions made during brainstorming, each with its rationale.

## Scope
**In Scope** — bullet list of what this covers.
**Out of Scope** — bullet list of what this explicitly does NOT cover.

## Test Strategy
What to test, test types, blockers vs nice-to-have.

## Open Questions
Anything unresolved that the planning/implementation phases need to address.
